## Your collection. Your infrastructure. Your rules.

Araru organizes and serves a digital collection without handing control of your files, reading history, or accounts to a commercial platform. Run the Server where you choose and connect official or third-party clients through its API.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Private by design" icon="lock">Your database, storage, credentials, and reading state remain on infrastructure you control.</Card>
  <Card title="One server, many clients" icon="seti:network">Araru Server is the source of truth. Web, Android, and Desktop clients consume the same API.</Card>
  <Card title="Built for real collections" icon="open-book">PDF, EPUB, MOBI, CBZ, and CBR readers, hierarchical folders, metadata, covers, search, and progress.</Card>
  <Card title="Open source" icon="github">Independent repositories, transparent architecture, AGPL licensing, and contribution-friendly documentation.</Card>
</CardGrid>

## The ecosystem

**Araru Server** owns authentication, catalog, metadata, search, progress, jobs, and content delivery. **Araru Web** is the current official client. Android and Desktop repositories are reserved for future clients and are not advertised as finished products.

## Start in minutes

Use Docker Compose to run PostgreSQL, Redis, Server, and Web, complete the first-time setup, and point Araru at your library. [Read the quick start →](/docs/getting-started/)
