Araru is an open-source, self-hosted digital library ecosystem. This documentation covers the current Server and Web products, operational concepts, architecture, and contribution workflow.

Araru OSS does not host, provide, distribute, sell, or maintain copies of any book, comic, document, or other file read by the software. Every installation is operated on infrastructure controlled by its user. The operator must supply legitimately acquired content, keep the original files and independent backups, and decide who may access the collection on their own network.

- [What is Araru?](./concepts/what-is-araru/): understand the product and its principles.
- [Content ownership and responsibility](./concepts/content-responsibility/): understand what the project and installation operator are responsible for.
